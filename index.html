Wallet Layer

A Solana wallet first. Wealth automation second.

Helio keeps the core wallet experience familiar: balances, sends, swaps, dapp approvals, and security checks. The difference is the optional add-on rule attached to every transaction.

01

Send and swap normally

Keep the transaction flow direct. Helio shows the payment, the add-on, the estimated network fee, and the total before signing.

02

Attach a small add-on

Use the default $0.50, $1.00, $1.50, or $2.00 rule. Larger custom limits can be configured by advanced users.

03

Route automatically

Pick where the add-on should go. Helio routes small amounts into the strategy you choose without turning every payment into a task.

04

Pause whenever needed

The add-on is always visible and always controlled by the user. Pause it, lower it, or change the route from the wallet surface.
